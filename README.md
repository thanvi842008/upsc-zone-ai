UPSC ZONE AI
Don't just read the paper. Answer it.
UPSC ZONE AI is a UPSC Civil Services preparation website that helps students turn current affairs into exam-oriented practice. With a newspaper-inspired editorial design, it encourages students to read news through the lens of the UPSC examiner and connect important issues to questions.
The website brings together daily news-style reading, subject-wise practice, saved clippings and revision notes, progress tracking, mock tests, and an overview of the UPSC exam journey from Prelims to Mains and Interview.
Note: Any illustrative stories or questions shown in the demo should be treated as sample content unless explicitly identified and verified as official material.
